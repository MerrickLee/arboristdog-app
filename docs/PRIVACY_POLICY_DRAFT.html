ARBORIST DOG · ALMSTEAD

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Arborist Dog is provided by Almstead Tree & Shrub Care Company (“Almstead,” “we,” “us”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Arborist Dog mobile app, including plant-health scans, account features, purchases, and requests for arborist services.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, account identifier, and sign-in information provided through your chosen sign-in method. With Sign in with Apple, you may use Apple’s private relay email. Linking a different email for customer recognition is optional and requires verification.
  • Photos and scan information: plant photos you capture or select, symptom selections, descriptions, and resulting diagnoses. Photos may incidentally include people or property; avoid including unnecessary personal or sensitive information.
  • Location and property information: ZIP code, address, property details, and location coordinates when you provide them or grant location permission. These help determine service availability and provide context for plant-health guidance.
  • Purchase information: product identifiers, transaction and subscription status, account identifiers, and scan-credit records. Apple processes App Store payments; the app does not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Service requests: contact details, property location, photo/diagnosis information, notes, and contact preferences you submit when requesting assistance.
  • Technical information: network and service logs, device/app details, and error information used to operate and secure the service. When product analytics is enabled, usage events, screen interactions, and an account identifier may be processed through Contentsquare/Heap.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts; save and display scan history; generate preliminary plant-health guidance; recognize verified Almstead customers; determine service-area benefits; verify purchases and subscriptions; maintain scan balances; respond to requested services and support; troubleshoot, protect, and improve the app; and meet applicable legal obligations.

AI analysis and service providers

When you submit a scan, your photo and the symptoms, description, and location context supplied with that scan are sent to Anthropic for AI analysis. AI guidance may be inaccurate and does not replace an on-site assessment by a qualified arborist. Do not include information you do not want processed for this purpose.

We use Supabase for authentication, databases, and photo storage; Apple and RevenueCat for purchases and subscription verification; and, when enabled, Contentsquare/Heap for product analytics. Your verified email may be checked against Almstead’s customer system to determine eligibility for customer benefits. Service requests may be routed through Zapier and Almstead’s customer-management systems, including GoHighLevel, to the staff handling your request.

Service providers process information to support these functions under their applicable service and privacy terms. We require providers handling personal information on our behalf to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable requirements. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and security, in connection with a business transfer, or at your direction. We do not sell personal information or share it with third parties for their independent marketing.

Your choices

You can manage camera, photo-library, and location permissions in your device settings. You can choose which photos to submit and use Apple’s Hide My Email feature. Declining a permission may limit the corresponding feature. Subscription cancellation is managed through your Apple account; deleting the app or requesting account deletion does not automatically cancel a subscription.

Retention, deletion, and security

We retain account information and scan history while needed to provide your account and services. Purchase records, support records, and limited logs may be kept as necessary for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal requirements. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account and associated information by contacting us below and identifying Arborist Dog. We may verify your identity before responding. Information required for legitimate legal or security purposes and copies in backups may remain for the applicable retention period.

We use access controls and other safeguards, but no online system is completely secure. Uploaded photos are currently stored with public-link access: anyone who obtains a photo’s direct URL may be able to view it. Avoid submitting confidential images. Information may be processed in countries where our providers operate, including outside your home jurisdiction.

Children

Arborist Dog is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information, contact us so we can address it.
